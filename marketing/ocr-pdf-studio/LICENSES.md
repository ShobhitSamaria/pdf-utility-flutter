<https://www.remotion.dev/license> for the current terms.

4.0.463, as required by Remotion.

`whoosh.wav`, `tick.wav` and `impact.wav` were synthesized from scratch for this

## 8. Third-party dependencies

The npm dependency tree is recorded in `video/package-lock.json`. Most packages
are MIT; TypeScript is Apache-2.0; a few carry ISC or BSD licenses. `npm audit`
currently reports inherited vulnerabilities from the Appshot template
dependency tree. That is a security-hygiene issue, not a licensing issue, and it
was left unchanged to avoid altering the template’s pinned versions.
